# Current data and execution audit — 2026-10-12

## Directly inspected

The current files contain **2,539,608 training spectra** (18 columns) and **1,213
test spectra** (12 columns), representing **400 test molecules**. Training has
275,810 supplied connectivity keys, 277,566 SMILES, and 51,291 molecular formulas.
Supplied keys are provenance; scorer identities must be recomputed with RDKit.

Train columns: ingest_lib, normalized_smiles, inchikey, inchikey14,
molecular_formula, ionization_mode, instrument_type, adduct, adduct_orig,
precursor_mz, precursor_error_ppm, ms2_mzs, ms2_normalized_intensities, num_peaks,
base_peak_intensity, collision_energy_ev, collision_energy_orig,
collision_energy_orig_units. Test replaces structure/source fields with molecule_id
and spectrum_id and lacks molecular formula and precursor error.

Spectra are variable-length float64 m/z and intensity lists. Converted collision
energies are float64 lists; original energies are strings. Precursor m/z and
adducts are available; there is no test formula or MS1 isotope envelope.
Metadata distributions and every scalar-column missing count were regenerated in
`notes/data_statistics.json`; full-array statistics are generated by
`scripts/inspect_current_data.py` into `notes/current_data_audit.json`.

Train precursor m/z: median 350.1088, 99th percentile 976.3292, maximum 2,430,333.
The extreme tail needs source/adduct quality filtering. Visible-test precursor
m/z: 245.0931–460.1554, median 329.1978. Training nulls include instrument_type
33,053; precursor_error_ppm 4,286; base_peak_intensity 1,163,138; original
collision energy 320,464. Structure, formula, adduct, and precursor fields have
zero scalar nulls. Test scalar fields have zero nulls.

The fresh full-array scan completed: zero mismatched peak/intensity lengths and
zero nonfinite peak-array rows in either file. Train converted-energy lists are
missing in **337,443** rows; test has none missing. Train peak counts have median
42 and maximum 73,318; test median 230 and maximum 3,259. Token limits and peak
selection therefore need explicit validation rather than an assumed small spectrum.

For 276,940 unique SMILES records with supported neutral formula syntax, median
heavy-atom count is 23, 95th percentile 41, maximum 238. Observed elements include
C, H, N, O, F, Cl, P, S, Br, I, As, B, Si, Se, Sn. **626** formula records carry
syntax such as a charge suffix and were excluded from this neutral inventory;
they must not be silently treated as neutral. The experimental DBE filter supports
CHNOS/halogens only and is deliberately incomplete for this dataset. **1,612**
supplied connectivity keys have multiple SMILES records; this is not by itself
evidence of distinct scorer identities. The sample submission has 400 unique IDs,
exactly matches test IDs, has zero nulls, and contains 25 guesses per row.

## Metric and leakage

The [official evaluation](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/enveda-CASMI26-molecule-id-mass-spectra)
uses molecule-level MRR@25 after RDKit 2026.03.3 tautomer canonicalization and
first-block InChIKey comparison. `src/metric.py` implements the published procedure,
preserves ranks of invalid guesses, and truncates at 25. Private scorer code is
unavailable, so exact behavior on undocumented malformed inputs is unverified.
Submission columns are molecule_id and smiles; guesses are semicolon-separated.

The previous audit documents visible-test training overlap and the official
placeholder policy. That overlap has **not been independently recomputed in this
run**. Never use visible-test matching as hidden-test performance. Existing
validation excludes held molecular identities globally and supports random
structure/scaffold/domain splits. Historical metrics are development results,
not fresh measurements or an untouched final holdout. Canonical duplicate,
fingerprint-neighbor, molecular-mass and valence audits require working RDKit.

## Current implementation and blocker

evaluation, and packaging were reviewed rather than duplicated. This checkout
initially omitted candidate/structure-map Parquets, library peak cache, and trained
weights referenced by the README. Metadata was rebuilt successfully.

A local `.venv` now contains the pinned requirements. Windows application control
blocks RDKit's rdCIPLabeler native DLL. The error persists outside the sandbox.
Both existing and new chemistry test modules fail at import; **no chemistry tests
passed and no fresh baseline score is claimed**. PyTorch is also absent. Syntax
compilation passed. The independent candidate-index test passed, including ppm
boundaries and fallback-versus-coverage behavior. `notes/current_preflight.json`
records prerequisite checks.

Added experimental bounded neutral-formula enumeration with ppm matching, DBE
pruning and explicit budget failures, plus one/two-cut connected fragment inventories
and ambiguous peak matching. Parent hydrogen inventories are preserved instead of
silently saturating broken valences. Ring opening alone is not counted as a detached
fragment. These hypotheses do not model all ion rearrangements, metal adducts,
isotope-labeled molecules, or general charge states; unsupported cases fail or are
explicitly skipped. They remain outside production inference pending tests and
validation. `scripts/enumerate_fragments.py` caches a bounded pilot and reports
one/two-cut peak and intensity coverage when RDKit is usable.
